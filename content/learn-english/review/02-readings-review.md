## R16 · From Receptionist to Programmer — من موظفة استقبال إلى مبرمجة

**النص الإنجليزي:**

> Five years ago, Amal was working as a receptionist in a small hotel. She answered the phone, booked rooms and wrote notes by hand. She liked her colleagues, but she felt that her salary would never change and that she was learning nothing new. One evening, while she was watching a video on the internet, she discovered free programming lessons. At first the words on the screen looked like a secret language. She decided to study for one hour every night after work, even when she was tired. Sometimes she failed the same exercise five times before she understood it. After eight months she built a simple website for the hotel, and guests started to book rooms online. The manager was impressed and asked her to take care of all the hotel technology. Two years later, Amal left the hotel and joined a small company that creates programmes for hospitals. Today she earns three times more than before, and every Friday she teaches coding to a group of women in her neighbourhood for free. She says that the most difficult step was the first one.
